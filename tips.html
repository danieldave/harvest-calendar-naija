💡 Farming Basics: How to Plant Right

Simple lessons every new farmer should know before planting. Read them, then check the calendar for your crop.

📆 Month view 📊 Year chart 🌦️ By season
🔍
    🌧️

    Wait for steady rains

    Don't plant after the first shower. Many extension officers advise waiting until the rains are regular and the soil is moist to about a hand's depth, so seedlings don't die in a dry spell.

    🌱

    Use certified seed

    Buy improved, certified seeds and clean planting materials (stems, setts, suckers). Many diseases, like cassava mosaic, travel in infected planting material.

    🔄

    Rotate your crops

    Follow cereals like maize or sorghum with legumes like cowpea, soybean or groundnut. Legumes add nitrogen and break disease and Striga cycles.

    🧺

    Harvest & store well

    Dry grains well before storage and keep them off the floor in airtight (hermetic) bags. Good drying is the main defence against aflatoxin moulds.

    🧪

    Use chemicals safely

    Only use registered pesticides, follow the label exactly, wear protection and keep them away from children and food.

    👩🏾‍🌾

    Ask your ADP

    Every state has an Agricultural Development Programme (ADP) with extension officers who know your local planting dates and varieties.

    Important: Planting and harvest months on this site are general guides drawn from Nigerian research and extension sources (IITA, NAERLS, NRCRI, NCRI, NIHORT, CRIN, NIFOR, IAR and FAO). Dates change with location, variety, irrigation and each year's rainfall. Always verify local dates with your state ADP or a trained extension officer.
