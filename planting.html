🌧️ Is it time to plant?

Pick your state and crop. We check the rain of the last 14 days and the forecast for the next 16 days, the way extension officers do.

How we decide

  1. Right place? Does this crop grow in your part of Nigeria?
  2. Right season? Is this month in the crop's planting window?
  3. Rains started? Scientists say the rains have truly started when 20 mm falls within 3 days.
  4. Dry spell coming? 10 or more dry days in a row can kill young seedlings, so we warn you.
  5. Soil, flood and heat: we check topsoil moisture, very heavy rain and very hot days.

This is a guide, not a promise. Weather is checked at your state capital. Always confirm with your ADP extension officer.
